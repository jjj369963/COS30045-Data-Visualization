Comparing the energy use of common TV models in the Australian market.

Background

Televisions are one of the most common appliances in Australian homes. Modern flat-screen TVs are generally more efficient than older CRT models, but screen size and brightness settings still have a significant effect on power draw.

Tips to Reduce TV Energy Use

  • Lower the screen brightness — reducing it by 30–50% cuts power draw without much effect on picture quality.
  • Turn the TV off at the wall instead of leaving it on standby. Standby mode still draws 1–5 W continuously.
  • Set a sleep timer so the TV switches off automatically if you fall asleep or leave the room.
  • When buying a new TV, check the star rating. A 5-star model uses much less energy than a 2-star model of the same size.
